Who we are

One team, multiple ways of finding what's underneath

Arrosho was built on a simple observation: whether you're a staffing agency short on pipeline or a builder short on land, the actual problem is the same — you need someone doing the finding and solving, not just the asking and theorizing.

Arrosho is built around the method, not the industry: trace the stream to its source, and bring back something worth building on.

"Arrosho" takes its name from arroyo — a brook, a stream. Water that finds its own path below the surface, carving a route to opportunity that isn't sitting on the open market or the open inbox.

That's the throughline across everything we do, for every client, in every conversation.
