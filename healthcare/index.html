The Direct Facility Pipeline

The right facility, at the right moment, for work you can fill.

Step one

Match the facilities to your bench

The mistake is buying a list of every hospital in the state and blasting it. A meeting is worthless if you can't fill what they need.

So I start with you: the roles you fill well, the facility types, the markets. That's also how you stop sounding like every other agency.

Step two

Time it

I look for signs of pressure first: roles that keep getting reposted, a new unit opening, a new director of nursing, gaps in a hard-to-fill specialty.

I reach the people who influence staffing, and I screen out the VMS-only and exclusive-vendor facilities I can spot.

Step three

Land in the inbox

Cold email that lands in spam never gets read, and nothing tells you it happened.

I set up separate sending domains so your main domain stays out of it, configure them properly, and warm them up for weeks before a single prospect email goes out.

Step four

Start a conversation, then qualify it

Short, plain emails that ask for something small. When a facility says yes, they get a short video from your agency. I help you script it.

Before anything goes on your calendar, it's checked against the five questions below.

What counts

A meeting only counts if all five are yes

  1. Does the facility match your facility types, size, roles, and markets, and can you work with them directly, with no VMS wall, procurement lockout, or exclusive vendor?
  2. Is this person involved in staffing decisions?
  3. Do they have a real staffing need, now or coming soon?
  4. Do they know they're meeting a staffing agency, and why?
  5. Did they accept the invite themselves, and actually show up?

You see my notes on every meeting, and you get five business days to dispute any of them. No-shows don't count. A cancelled call gets rebooked, and it counts once it happens.

Fit

Who this is for, and who it isn't

This is for you if

  • You own a small or midsize healthcare staffing agency.
  • You have a niche you're good at, and a bench of clinicians or a reliable way to find and credential them.
  • You can fill more than you're being asked to fill.
  • You have at least one placement or facility result you can point to.
  • Someone on your team can take the meetings and follow up fast.

It's not for you if

  • You can't fill the orders you already have. More meetings would just show the problem to more people. Fix delivery first.
  • Every facility you serve buys only through a VMS.
  • You want a list of contacts, not conversations.
