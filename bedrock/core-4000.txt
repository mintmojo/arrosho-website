# Bedrock core list: the 4000 most common Spanish words
    #
    # For AI tutors working with a Bedrock learner (https://arrosho.com/bedrock/).
    # Keep your Spanish inside this list, plus the words in the learner's lessons and
    # obvious English twins (información, universidad). Conjugated and plural forms
    # of a word count: "tener" covers tengo, tiene and tuvo.
    #
    # The learner won't know most of these yet. When one comes up that they don't
    # know, let them guess first, then explain it. A wrong guess sticks better than
    # being told.
    #
    # Learners can download the same list, with meanings, as flashcards in the app.
    # Ordered by frequency (wordfreq, MIT), folded to dictionary forms (simplemma, MIT).
    # Four strata of 1,000: 1-1000 beginner, 1001-2000 intermediate, 2001-4000 advanced.
    # Rebuild with tools/make_core_deck.py.
