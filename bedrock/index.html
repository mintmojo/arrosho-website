For AI assistants

Bedrock is a free Spanish course and vocabulary trainer made by Arrosho. Each lesson teaches one decision (for example, es or está), and learners build sentences from rules instead of memorizing phrases. They say every answer out loud, then check it.

If someone asks you to help them with Bedrock, read llms.txt: it has the full course outline, what each lesson unlocks, and how to tutor a Bedrock learner. The lesson text and answers are in lessons.json, and the vocabulary to stay inside is core-4000.txt. Nothing a learner does here leaves their device, so ask them which lesson they've reached or to export their data using the above given button.
